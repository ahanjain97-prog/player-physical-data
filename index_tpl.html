How to read this

Tracking baselines shift between seasons and between leagues by more than most players shift, so a raw number from 2025 cannot be compared with one from 2026. Percentiles inside a single league-season-position pool are the only stable unit here, and even those carry real noise.

Trust these
  • Distance Per 90 and Meters Per Minute are the most reproducible figures in the file.
  • Everything is a per-90 rate, so it is not distorted by minutes played.
  • All pools shown are 20+ players unless the card says otherwise.
Read with care
  • Sprint distance and count swing about 25–35% for the same player between seasons.
  • Acceleration and deceleration counts are effort counts, not a measure of how explosive a player is.
  • Team style leaks in — a side that plays end-to-end lifts everyone's volume.
Do not lean on these
  • Max Speed is a single-match peak. Year-over-year correlation within a position group is about 0.2, and the league-wide median rose roughly 1.2 km/h in 2026 across all three leagues at once — a measurement change, not players getting faster.
  • MLS NEXT Pro 2024 covers only 26% of that season's players. Treat it as indicative only.
  • Cross-league comparison of raw values. Use percentiles, or nothing.
